# Reading Log

**A reading dashboard for KOReader's home screens, [Simple UI](https://github.com/doctorhetfield-cmd/simpleui.koplugin) and [Bookshelf](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin), made for colour e-ink.**

Activity rings, monthly and yearly charts, your longest reads, the time of
day you read, a wall of every cover you finished this year, a book club
tracker, reading trends like Apple's Fitness app, this week against last, a
monthly challenge, library stats, a random pick from your unread books, a
quote from your highlights, a cover calendar of every day you read, a
control centre for your light and a device card for the battery, storage and
memory, and five ornaments that hang on the wallpaper or stand on
Bookshelf's shelf (the year's books, a streak flame, a letter board with
your own words, today's pages, every page you've turned), and Lifetime
pages, every page you've ever turned as a card for the Dashboard. That's 43 cards,
plus a reader panel under
the reader's menu, a Dashboard that puts
them together as tabbed pages (Today, Yearly, Monthly, Trends, Library and
Calendar, and your own), and a Mini dashboard for the top of the home screen.

One plugin works with both home screens: install it, and the cards show up in
Simple UI's and in Bookshelf's module lists. Made on, and for, a Kobo Libra
Colour.

The ring designs and the idea of a reading dashboard come from
[u/fishnoogz](https://www.reddit.com/user/fishnoogz/)'s Reddit post.

## What you need

- [KOReader](https://koreader.rocks) with either home screen:
  - [Simple UI](https://github.com/doctorhetfield-cmd/simpleui.koplugin) **2.7.5 or newer**, or
  - [Bookshelf](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin) **6.0 or newer** (it loads the cards from the plugin).
- KOReader's **Reading statistics** plugin switched on (it is by default).
  All the reading figures come from it.
- A colour screen is optional. On a black-and-white screen the colours show
  as greys.

## Install

**With KOReader's App Store plugin:** search for Reading Log and install it.

**By hand:**

1. Download `readinglog-1.4.0.zip` from [Releases](../../releases) and unzip it.
2. Copy the `readinglog.koplugin` folder into KOReader's `plugins` folder:
   - Kobo: `.adds/koreader/plugins/` (`.adds` is hidden: in macOS Finder press
     <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>.</kbd>; in Windows Explorer choose
     View > Show > Hidden items)
   - Kindle: `/mnt/us/koreader/plugins/`
   - Android: `<koreader folder>/plugins/`
3. Eject the e-reader and restart KOReader.

Your settings live in `koreader/settings/readinglog.lua`, outside the plugin
folder, so updates keep them.

### Coming from an older version

- **The Bookshelf micro-modules version (1.3 and before):** install the
  plugin as above. On the next start Reading Log offers to **Remove** the old
  copy in `settings/bookshelf/micromodules` (Bookshelf would keep using it
  otherwise), then asks you to restart. Your settings are copied across, and
  your Bookshelf layouts keep their cards, since the cards have the same
  names. To do it by hand instead, delete the `alm_*.lua` files and the
  `almanac` folder from that folder.
- **Reading Log for Simple UI:** replace the `readinglog.koplugin` folder with
  the new one (or use Check for updates). Nothing else changes.

## Updates

KOReader menu > Tools > **Reading Log** > **Check for updates** looks for a
newer release here, shows what's new, and installs it when you say so
(connect to Wi-Fi first, or KOReader will offer to). Restart KOReader
afterwards. Turn on **Check for updates automatically** to have it look once a
day when you're already online; it never installs anything without asking.
The App Store plugin can update it too.

## With Simple UI

**Add cards:** Simple UI Settings > **Home Screen** > **Layout Editor**, open a
page, tap **Add Module**, and pick any `Reading Log · …` module. Set a card's
Column Width to 50% (long-press > Column Width) to put two side by side.

**The Dashboard** is meant to fill a screen on its own:

1. Simple UI Settings > **Extra Custom Screens**: create one (call it
   "Reading Log", say). Simple UI gives it a Quick Action, so you can put it
   on the navigation bar.
2. Open its layout and add **Reading Log · Dashboard** on its own.
3. Long-press the Dashboard and set **Top Margin** to 0 and **Height** to
   Fill the page.

Long-press any card for its settings: **Content** (the card's own options),
**Appearance** (card height, text size, card colour, Look and colours) and
**When tapped** (the card's own action, the reading calendar, reading
progress, or any Simple UI Quick Action). Look and colours is also in
Tools > Reading Log.

With a Simple UI wallpaper on, the Dashboard follows its **Background
Opacity**: below Solid, the wallpaper shows around and between the cards.
A card on its own that draws its own frame (Cyber, Dark Academia) and the
ornaments sit straight on the wallpaper, without Simple UI's module box
round them.

Shared with Simple UI: the year's book goal and the daily minutes goal are
its Reading goals, books finished are counted the way it counts them,
covers come from its cover cache, and Details opens its book menu.

## With Bookshelf

**The Dashboard** is meant to fill Bookshelf's full-screen view on its own.

1. Bookshelf menu > Settings > Micro modules: turn on **Full-screen button**.
2. Tap that button, at the bottom right of the Bookshelf home screen, to open
   the full-screen view.
3. Long-press a module there to open the layout editor, tap **+ Add** and add
   **Reading Log · Dashboard** (the Reading Log cards have a group of their
   own in the list).
4. Make it the biggest size, and remove every other module from the view.
   The Dashboard then fills the screen.

To use single cards instead, add any `Reading Log · …` module next to
Bookshelf's own, in the full-screen view or the home screen's top area.
Long-press a card > **Module settings** for its settings.

Shared with Bookshelf: the year's book goal, books read outside KOReader and
the daily minutes goal are its Reading goals settings, Quote of the day is
the same daily quote as its own, and covers and book facts come from its
library.

## The Dashboard

Tap the tabs at the bottom to switch pages. The Dashboard's pages can be
changed too: long-press it > **Pages and cards**. You can add pages of your
own there (up to eight tabs), and **Put the whole Dashboard back as it came**
in the Dashboard's settings undoes every change.

| Page | What's on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Banner, Continue reading, Activity rings beside Week rings, Book club |
| **Yearly** | Time read by month beside Longest reads (year), Books and pages, Year in covers |
| **Monthly** | Month rings beside Month totals, Pages per day, When you read beside Longest reads (month) |
| **Trends** | Monthly challenge beside Finish forecast, Reading trends |
| **Library** | Library stats, Random pick beside Most-read authors, Quote of the day |
| **Calendar** | Cover calendar: every day of the month with the cover of the book you read |

On the Monthly page, the big arrows at the top step back one month at a time
(up to twelve months), and a **Today** button brings you back. Every card on
the page follows.

## The reader panel

Off until you switch it on: Tools > Reading Log > **Reader panel** > **Show
it when the reader's menu opens**. Then the reader's top menu opens on a
small panel instead of the first tab's list: the book's cover, title and
author, then two lines of figures. The first has the page, percent, time
left, the time spent in this book and the days you've read it; the second
has today's pages, time read and your streak, in the colours of today's
activity rings beside them. A thin progress bar, and a row of buttons. The
arrow, time and battery stay along the bottom.

Each part can be switched off there (the book, today's figures, this book's
figures, the rings), and this week's rings switched on. Three buttons
as it comes (Wi-Fi,
bookmarks and the light), up to eight, each with any action KOReader or
another plugin offers (picked from the same list as gestures). Each button's
icon can be one of Reading Log's glyphs, one of KOReader's own icons, or a
picture of your own (PNG, JPEG or SVG). The buttons follow your style:
rounded keys for Default, pastel pills for Cute, cut-corner keys for Cyber and
brass for Dark Academia. Tap the open tab to come back to the panel from a
tab's list; hold a button to see what it does.

## The Mini dashboard

For the top of the home screen: one row of up to three small cards. As it
comes it shows the book you're reading, with a small row of today's rings
under its progress (pages, minutes and streak), then the Control centre. In
its settings, pick up to three (Reading and today's rings, Control centre,
Continue reading, Activity rings, This week vs last, Week rings, Monthly
challenge, Finish forecast, Year goal, Today's timeline, Random pick, Quote of
the day, Library stats, Week in covers). It shows as many as fit, left to
right.

## Languages

Reading Log follows KOReader's interface language. It comes in English,
Italian, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese (also used for Portuguese
from Portugal): pick the language in KOReader's language menu and restart,
and the cards, Dashboard and settings follow. Other languages fall back to
English. See [Translating Reading Log](docs/TRANSLATING.md) to add another
language.

## What each module does

All the modules are called `Reading Log · …` in the home screen's module list.

### Dashboard

**Dashboard** puts the cards below together as tabbed pages (Today, Yearly,
Monthly, Trends, Library, Calendar) that fill the full-screen view. You can pick the
cards on each page, their order, side by side or full width, and their
height, add pages of your own, change the order of the tabs, and put it all
back as it came.

**Style: Cute** (in the Dashboard's settings, or Look and colours on any
card) turns on every cute look at once: hearts for the rings, a flower for
the Monthly challenge, a garden for the Book club, a jar of hearts for the
Year goal, notebook paper for the quote cards, polaroids for the cover
calendars, a scrapbook for Continue reading, sticky notes for Reading
trends, book spines and hearts for the charts, a sky for Today's timeline,
a desk calendar for the Finish forecast, a heart for the Device card's
battery, hearts for Lifetime pages, and for the ornaments a heart sticker, a heart on fire, a light box,
a sticky note and a pink banner. **Cyber** does the same with
the cyber looks, on a black page in neon with cut corners: segmented rings,
a HUD for Continue reading, a data stream for the Book club, a terminal for
the Year goal, a neural network for the Monthly challenge, a terminal for the quote
cards, brackets for the cover calendars, a diagnostics readout for Reading
trends, neon charts, a skyline for Today's timeline, a countdown for the
Finish forecast, a system monitor for the Device card, a data counter for
Lifetime pages, and for the ornaments a glitch billboard, a neon flame, an LED marquee, a heart
monitor and a data counter.
**Dark Academia**
does the same with its looks, on
parchment in a serif: pocket watches for the rings, a gilt-framed cover with a fountain pen,
a ledger for the Book club, a globe for the Year goal, a chess board for the
Monthly challenge, a manuscript page for the quotes, gilt-framed calendars,
catalogue cards, candles, a gallery wall for Year in covers, a celestial
chart, an almanac, a candle burning down for the battery, a ledger for
Lifetime pages, and for the ornaments a brass plaque, a candle, a chalkboard, a library card and an
open book.
**Default**
puts them all back (and your
theme, after Cyber or Dark Academia).

## Make it yours

Long-press any card for its settings (in Bookshelf, **Module settings**):

- **The card's own options:** goals, collections, how many books and so on.
- **When tapped:** keep the card's own action, or run something else instead:
  with Simple UI, the reading calendar, reading progress or any Quick Action;
  with Bookshelf, a plugin, a KOReader action or a menu entry, picked with its
  action chooser. Buttons on a card keep their own job.
- **Card colour:** plain or tinted, and which of the two accent colours leads.
- **Dashboard pages:** add your own pages, rename or hide any page, move the
  tabs, or put the whole Dashboard back as it came.
- **Look and colours** (all cards at once): theme (White, Dark, Paper, Cyber
  or Dark Academia), Style (Default, Cute, Cyber or Dark Academia), two accent
  colours from sixteen, how cards look on their own (matching the home screen
  or framed in the theme), the day the week starts on, and Diagnostics.

Times follow KOReader's own 12- or 24-hour clock (Settings > Time and date).

The [guide](docs/GUIDE.md) covers every setting, how the rings and When you
read are worked out, and setting up the book club.

## Troubleshooting

- KOReader menu > Tools > Reading Log > **About** shows the version and
  whether Simple UI and Bookshelf were found.
- Look and colours > **Diagnostics** shows the versions, theme and screen.
- No cards in Bookshelf? It needs version 6.0 or newer, and an old copy in
  `settings/bookshelf/micromodules` takes their place until it is removed.
- Problems are logged to `crash.log` with `[readinglog]`.
